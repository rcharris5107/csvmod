FAQ

Do I pay for my end users' imports?

Yes — that's the point of usage pricing, but only delivered rows count. At Pro the first 100k are included; 4¢/row after.

Can I use my own AI key?

Yes. Bring your DeepSeek key and CsvMod routes to it — useful for data residency or if you want DeepSeek's cache pricing on high volume.

Do I have to host your cloud?

No. The embed is a single JS file. Self-host it, or use our CDN. Batch automation via OpenClaw can run on your own infrastructure.
